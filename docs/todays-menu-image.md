# Sample menu asset

`public/images/sample-menu.png` is the illustrative menu poster used by the public demo. It lists the 23 dishes in `src/lib/sample-catalog.ts`: six breakfast dishes, eleven lunch dishes and six salads.

The poster and dish photographs were generated for the demo. They do not represent an actual café posting or verified ingredient information. The poster asks visitors to confirm ingredients and allergens with the café.

The image-generation subjects and shared photography prompt are recorded in [Asset provenance](curated-preview-assets.json). These assets are bundled independently of private menu uploads and the application database.
